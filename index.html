FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

Is it necessary to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

You can create a basic short link without an account. Sign in to manage your links, customize them, and see their performance.

Are the shortened links permanent? Will they ever expire?

Links remain available while their account and destination are active. Account settings and service terms may affect availability.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

Link limits depend on your plan. Review the pricing options above for the features included with each plan.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Yes. Add a custom alias and, on supported plans, use your own branded domain for shareable links.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Link analytics can show engagement and help you understand how people interact with your links.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are the shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

Links should be shared responsibly. Security and abuse protections depend on the service and its policies.

What is a QR code and what can it do?

A QR code stores information such as a URL and can be scanned with a phone camera to open that destination.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my own applications or websites?

API access is available on eligible plans. See the Teams plan or contact us for details.
